FloppoScope parses raw sector images in your browser. It does not modify the image. FAT (including Atari ST and MSX-DOS disks), SunOS Bar/Tar archives, NeXT UFS1, SCO UNIX System V (S5), classic Macintosh MFS/HFS, Apple Lisa PREBOOT boot-file volumes, Commodore CBM DOS, Atari DOS 2, AmigaDOS, Apple DOS 3.3/ProDOS, TR-DOS, Acorn DFS, DEC Files-11 ODS-1 (RSX-11), Sharp MZ Disk BASIC, Sharp MZ-800 Personal CP/M-80, Sharp MZF/MZT tape files and VTech Laser/VZ200 DOS are inspected read-only. MSA, ATR, XFD and 2IMG wrappers are unwrapped automatically. SCP, KryoFlux, HFE, WOZ (5.25-inch), NIB, G64 and IMD images are decoded to sectors when a supported sector format is found; IPF, DMS, TD0 and FDI are recognised but not decoded. Amstrad CPC / Extended CPC DSK containers are unwrapped to a flat sector image first (as used for Sharp MZ disks). VZ200 .dsk / .dvz disk-drive images (98,560 to 99,185 bytes, early and corrected layouts) are rebuilt into a flat 40 × 16 × 128-byte image by their sector sync marks, and text-mode damage (CR LF expansion, or every 0x0D byte deleted) is repaired where the checksums allow; sectors whose lost 0x0D bytes cannot be placed are flagged. Macintosh 400K/800K GCR track zones and 1.44MB HD MFM geometry are shown when a supported volume is detected.

IMD and HFE files are rebuilt from the decoded sectors with standard MFM timing — they are not raw flux captures, so copy-protection timing is not preserved. Bad or missing sectors are flagged (IMD error records / HFE data CRC errors). Sources that are already IMD, HFE or Extended CPC DSK are copied unchanged. DSK output keeps each track's sector IDs, physical order and sizes (decoded flux and CPC DSK sources); for other images it is built from the C/H/S geometry. A CPC DSK source keeps its per-track layout in IMD/HFE too (e.g. a 16 × 256 B boot track next to 8 × 512 B tracks). Disks that are not PC-style MFM (Amiga, Apple, C64, Mac…) are skipped for IMD/HFE and listed in the manifest.
