FloppoScope parses raw sector images in your browser. It does not modify the image. FAT (including Atari ST and MSX-DOS disks), SunOS Bar/Tar archives, NeXT UFS1, SCO UNIX System V (S5), classic Macintosh MFS/HFS, Apple Lisa PREBOOT boot-file volumes, Commodore CBM DOS, Atari DOS 2, AmigaDOS, Apple DOS 3.3/ProDOS, TR-DOS and Acorn DFS are inspected read-only. MSA, ATR, XFD and 2IMG wrappers are unwrapped automatically. SCP, KryoFlux, HFE, WOZ (5.25-inch), NIB, G64 and IMD images are decoded to sectors when a supported sector format is found; IPF, DMS, TD0, FDI and Amstrad CPC DSK are recognised but not decoded. Macintosh 400K/800K GCR track zones and 1.44MB HD MFM geometry are shown when a supported volume is detected.

IMD and HFE files are rebuilt from the decoded sectors with standard MFM timing — they are not raw flux captures, so copy-protection timing is not preserved. Bad or missing sectors are flagged (IMD error records / HFE data CRC errors). Sources that are already IMD or HFE are copied unchanged. Disks that are not PC-style MFM (Amiga, Apple, C64, Mac…) are skipped for IMD/HFE and listed in the manifest.
